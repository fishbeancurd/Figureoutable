Questions

Good to know

Is Pawgress free?+

Yes. Everything in the app is free while we're in early access. Photo answers in the chat use your own AI account, which bills a few cents per question.

Does it replace my vet?+

No. Targets and tips follow widely published veterinary guidance and breed averages, and they're estimates for typical dogs. Pawgress points you to your vet for anything worrying and to emergency care for red-flag symptoms.

Which phones does it work on?+

iPhone and Android. Store versions are coming soon. Until then you can try the beta through the free Expo Go app.

Can I add more than one dog?+

Yes. Each dog gets its own profile, plan, logs, lessons and chat, and shared tasks like walks can be logged for several dogs at once.

Do I need an account?+

No. Everything is saved on your phone. Export a backup from your profile to move to a new phone.

How are calories worked out?+

Pawgress uses resting energy (70 × weight in kg to the power of 0.75) multiplied by a life-stage factor: about 3 for puppies under 4 months, 2 while growing, 1.6 to 1.8 for adults and about 1.4 for seniors, adjusted for energy level. It updates as your dog grows and gains weight.
